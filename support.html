Common questions

Camera permission is off
iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → SafeScan. You can also scan from Photos or Files.

Restore Pro
Settings → Restore Purchases (same Apple ID used to buy).

History locked
Unlock with Face ID / Touch ID / passcode. History re-locks when you leave the app if lock is enabled.

Safety rating wrong?
Ratings are on-device heuristics (HTTP, IP hosts, shorteners, lookalike hints). Always verify before opening payment or login pages.
